How to play

Bombs wander the arena. Press and drag each one into the pen of the same color and let go. At least half the bomb must be over the pen.

A wrong pen, or a fuse hitting zero, means BOOM. Game over.

Powers: tap a power on the board to collect it as a card in the tray under the board. Copies stack on one card (up to 3). Tap a card to use it. Rarer powers show up as your score climbs. Black Hole, Defuse, Magnet and Paint then need a second tap on the board.

Freeze: stop every bomb and all spawns for 7s
Slow-mo: everything slows 10s
Defuse: tap up to 8 bombs, one by one, to remove them
Black Hole: tap a spot, swallows bombs +1 each
Magnet: tap a bomb, its color flies home 5s
Paint: tap a bomb, next 5 bombs match it
Fan: blows bombs to the middle
Swap: tap two pens to trade their places
Shield (rare): forgives one mistake
Auto-Sort (rare): sorts the whole board
x2 Points (rare): 2 per bomb for 10s
Rewind (rare): bombs go back 3s; new ones vanish

Rare powers are small, fast, flicker, and vanish in a few seconds. +1 per sorted bomb. Every 20 points the pens clear and a 15s event starts: Rush (more bombs, x2 points), Calm, Wind or Pen Swap.

Earn cards: sort quickly in a row for a combo. Combo sorts fill the bar under the board, and a full bar gives a free card. Each run also has 3 missions that pay a card each. Grab a bomb with almost no fuse left for a clutch save (+1).

Special bombs show up as you climb: Gift (bow: gives a card), Chameleon (rainbow ring: changes color every 3s), Heavy (slow to drag, short fuse) and Twin (worth 2 whole, splits in two if left). Every 100 points a Boss appears, one of four kinds: a plain one you tap apart, a Splitter that breaks into three small ones, a Color boss you beat by dragging bombs of the color it shows onto it, and a Thief that steals sorted bombs until you tap it out.

1v1 Race: two players join the same room code and get the same bombs. Every 3rd sort sends a junk bomb to your rival, who drags it into any pen. Last one standing wins.

Daily: Classic rules with the same bomb order for everyone today. A new seed every day.
